## ECDICT

`static/data/cet4-core.v1.json` is derived from CET-4-tagged entries in
[skywind3000/ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), pinned at commit
`bc015ed2e24a7abef49fc6dbbb7fe32c1dadaf8b`. Entries are ordered by ECDICT's
contemporary frequency rank and then BNC rank. Recall keeps only the word,
phonetic transcription, first Chinese translation line, and ranking metadata.
